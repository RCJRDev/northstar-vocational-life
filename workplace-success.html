Workplace Success

Practical soft skills training in communication, professionalism, and self-advocacy to help you keep the job and grow your career. Bilingual services available in English and Spanish.

What Workplace Success Training Helps With

Workplace success training is a collaborative partnership focused on helping you build the everyday skills that keep you employed, respected, and moving forward.

Communication

  • Talking clearly with supervisors and coworkers
  • Asking questions and receiving feedback well
  • Handling workplace conflict calmly and professionally

Professionalism

  • Reliability, punctuality, and workplace etiquette
  • Building better work habits and routines
  • Teamwork and adapting to workplace culture

Self-Advocacy & Confidence

  • Speaking up for what you need, including accommodations
  • Overcoming self-doubt on the job
  • Improving decision-making and self-discipline

Career Advancement

  • Positioning yourself for raises and promotions
  • Navigating role changes with confidence
  • Building a long-term career vision

Skills for the Workplace

Communication

Clearer, more confident interactions at work

Time Management

Increased productivity and reliability

Work-Life Balance

Stress management and well-being on the job

How Workplace Success Training Differs From Therapy

While both can be valuable, workplace success training and therapy serve different purposes and work best in different situations.

Aspect Workplace Success Training Therapy
Focus Future-focused, skills-oriented Often past/healing focused
Approach Action and performance oriented Mental health treatment
Diagnosis Does not diagnose disorders Can diagnose conditions
Primary Goal Job readiness and workplace performance Emotional healing
Best For Those who want practical workplace skills Those needing clinical support

Note: Workplace success training is not a substitute for therapy or medical treatment. If you're experiencing mental health challenges, please consult a licensed therapist.

What a Session Looks Like

Each training session is a focused conversation and practice designed to help you make measurable progress toward your workplace goals.

1. Check-In & Goal Review

We start with a conversation about your current workplace challenges, wins since our last session, and what you want to focus on today.

2. Skill-Building & Practice

Through role-play and guided practice, we build the specific communication, professionalism, or self-advocacy skill you're working on.

3. Strategy & Planning

Together, we identify practical strategies and create action steps tailored to your specific workplace situation and goals.

4. Action Steps for Next Week

You leave each session with clear, achievable action steps to try on the job before our next meeting, building momentum toward your goals.

5. Progress Review

At our next session, we review your progress, celebrate wins, address any obstacles, and continue moving forward.

Who Benefits Most from Workplace Success Training

Workplace success training is ideal for people who are ready to build practical skills and make positive changes in their work life.

New Employees

People starting a new job who want to build confidence, learn workplace norms, and get off to a strong start.

Employees Seeking Growth

Ambitious individuals who want to grow within their current job, earn a promotion, or reach the next level in their career.

Anyone Building Confidence

Anyone who wants better workplace habits, more confidence, improved relationships with coworkers, or a clearer sense of direction in their career.
