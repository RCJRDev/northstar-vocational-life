Vocational Counseling

Comprehensive career services to help you discover your path, prepare for success, and achieve your professional goals.

Our Vocational Services

With over 22 years of experience, I provide comprehensive vocational counseling services tailored to your unique situation, strengths, and goals.

Career Assessment

Understanding yourself is the first step to finding the right career path. Through comprehensive assessments, we explore multiple dimensions of who you are to identify careers that align with your unique profile.

  • Interests, strengths, and personality assessment
  • Values clarification and work style preferences
  • Skills inventory and experience evaluation
  • Aptitude and interest clarification

Discover the career paths that align with who you truly are.

Explore options you may never have considered.

Career Exploration

Once we understand your profile, we explore the world of work together to find occupations that match your interests and abilities.

  • Explore occupations and industries that match your profile
  • Research job requirements, salaries, and growth potential
  • Identify training and education options
  • Develop a clear career direction

Job Preparation

Having the right skills and presentation is crucial in today's competitive job market. I help you prepare every aspect of your job search.

  • Resume and cover letter development
  • Interview coaching and practice
  • Workplace skills and professional etiquette
  • Job search strategies and networking

Present your best self to potential employers.

Overcoming Barriers to Employment

With 22 years of experience working with individuals facing various challenges, I understand the unique obstacles that can make finding employment difficult.

Disabilities & Health Conditions

Specialized support for individuals with physical, cognitive, or mental health conditions seeking meaningful employment that accommodates their needs.

Career Transitions

Guidance for those recovering from job loss, changing industries, or returning to work after extended absence due to illness, caregiving, or other circumstances.

Training & Resources

Help navigating training funds, educational programs, vocational rehabilitation services, and other resources that can support your career goals.

Ongoing Career Development

Your career journey doesn't end when you land a job. I continue to support clients as they grow and evolve professionally.

Promotions & Advancement

Strategic guidance for climbing the career ladder, taking on new responsibilities, and positioning yourself for promotions.

Job Satisfaction

Address workplace challenges, improve work relationships, and find greater fulfillment in your current role.

Career Transitions

Navigate role changes, department moves, or industry shifts with confidence and strategic planning.

Long-Term Goals

Develop and work toward your long-term career vision, whether it's leadership, entrepreneurship, or a fulfilling retirement.
