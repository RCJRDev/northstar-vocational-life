Getting Hired

Practical job readiness training to help you discover your path, prepare a strong application, and land the job. Bilingual services available in English and Spanish.

Our Job Readiness Program

With over 22 years of experience, I provide job readiness training tailored to your unique situation, strengths, and goals.

Skills & Interest Assessment

Understanding yourself is the first step to finding the right job. Through practical assessments, we explore multiple dimensions of who you are to target jobs that fit your profile.

  • Interests, strengths, and personality assessment
  • Values clarification and work style preferences
  • Skills inventory and experience evaluation
  • Aptitude and interest clarification

Discover the jobs that align with who you truly are.

Explore options you may never have considered.

Job Search Strategy

Once we understand your profile, we explore the job market together to find openings that match your interests and abilities.

  • Explore occupations and industries that match your profile
  • Research job requirements, wages, and growth potential
  • Identify training and education options
  • Develop a clear job search plan

Resume, Interview & Application Prep

Having the right materials and presentation is crucial in today's competitive job market. I help you prepare every aspect of your job search.

  • Resume and cover letter development
  • Interview coaching and practice
  • Job applications and online profiles
  • Networking strategies and references

Present your best self to potential employers.

Overcoming Barriers to Employment

With 22 years of experience working with individuals facing various challenges, I understand the unique obstacles that can make finding employment difficult.

Disabilities & Health Conditions

Specialized support for individuals with physical, cognitive, or mental health conditions seeking meaningful employment that accommodates their needs.

Career Changes & Re-Entry

Guidance for those recovering from job loss, changing industries, or returning to work after extended absence due to illness, caregiving, or other circumstances.

Training & Resources

Help navigating training funds, educational programs, vocational rehabilitation services, and other resources that can support your job search.
