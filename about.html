About NorthStar

Dedicated to helping individuals discover their strengths, build independence, and move forward with confidence.

Your Guide to a Better Future

For over two decades, I've had the privilege of helping individuals navigate life's challenges and discover their professional potential.

With a Master's of Science Degree and 22 years of professional experience as a vocational counselor, I've worked with people across a wide spectrum of needs—from those just entering the workforce to seasoned professionals seeking new directions. Bilingual services are available in English and Spanish to better serve our diverse community.

My approach is grounded in a simple belief: everyone has unique strengths and abilities that can lead to meaningful work and a fulfilling life. My role is to help you discover those strengths and create a path forward that honors who you are.

My Approach

I believe in meeting you where you are and building from your strengths.

Values-Based

Your values guide our work together. Understanding what matters most to you helps us identify goals and paths that will bring genuine fulfillment.

Strengths-Focused

Rather than dwelling on limitations, we identify and build upon your unique strengths and abilities to create sustainable success.

Practical Strategies

Every session includes actionable strategies you can implement immediately. Real progress comes from taking consistent, practical steps.

Accountability

I provide the structure and support you need to follow through on your commitments and maintain momentum toward your goals.

Safe Environment

Our conversations are completely confidential, creating a safe space where you can explore options and work through challenges openly.

Holistic View

Career and life are interconnected. I consider all aspects of your situation to develop comprehensive solutions that support your overall well-being.

My Life Coaching Philosophy

"Everyone deserves the opportunity to discover their strengths, build independence, and move forward with confidence. My job is to help you see what's possible and support you as you create the life you want."

My life coaching approach is built on the same foundation as my vocational counseling work: a deep belief in each person's potential and a commitment to practical, results-oriented support.

  • Values and strengths-based — We build on what's already working
  • Practical strategies — Every session includes actionable steps
  • Accountability — Structured support to maintain momentum
  • Personalized guidance — Tailored to your unique situation
