About Pathways to Employment

Dedicated to preparing people for employment and workplace success — from getting the job to keeping the job.

Your Partner in Employment Success

For over two decades, I've had the privilege of helping people prepare for, land, and keep meaningful jobs.

With a Master's of Science Degree and 22 years of professional experience in vocational and workforce development, I've worked with people across a wide spectrum of needs — from first-time job seekers to professionals working toward advancement. Bilingual services are available in English and Spanish to better serve our diverse community.

My approach is grounded in a simple belief: employment success comes from practical, teachable skills — communication, professionalism, self-advocacy, and job readiness. My role is to help you build those skills and put them to work.

My Approach

I believe in meeting you where you are and building from your strengths.

Goal-Driven

Your job goals guide our work together. Understanding what you want helps us focus training on the skills that matter most.

Strengths-Focused

Rather than dwelling on limitations, we identify and build upon your unique strengths and abilities to create sustainable employment success.

Practical Strategies

Every session includes actionable skills you can use on your very next application, interview, or shift.

Accountability

I provide the structure and support you need to follow through on your job search and stay on track once you're hired.

Safe Environment

Our conversations are completely confidential, creating a safe space where you can practice skills and work through challenges openly.

Whole-Person View

Getting hired and keeping a job are interconnected. I consider your whole situation to develop solutions that actually stick.

Our Program Philosophy
